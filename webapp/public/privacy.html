Privacy Policy — Vibe Lifestyle

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Vibe Lifestyle (@Eza_Shop_Bot) is a Telegram Mini App online store. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Data we collect

  • Telegram profile: user ID, first name, username, and profile photo (when you open the shop).
  • Order information: phone number, delivery address, items ordered, payment method, and order notes you provide at checkout.
  • Shop activity: wishlist items, loyalty points, and last visit time.

2. How we use your data

  • Process and deliver your orders.
  • Send order updates via Telegram.
  • Improve shop experience and customer support.
  • Prevent fraud and enforce shop rules.

3. Data storage & security

Data is stored securely on encrypted servers (PostgreSQL). Phone numbers and addresses are encrypted at rest. Product images are hosted via Cloudinary.

4. Third parties

We do not sell your personal data. We use Telegram, Cloudinary, and hosting providers only to operate the shop.

5. Your rights

You may request access to or deletion of your data by contacting us through @Eza_Shop_Bot.
